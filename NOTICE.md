# PDN Domain Verification Policy

## Purpose

PDN Verification is an authenticity confirmation mechanism designed to establish a publicly verifiable connection between a domain NFT and the individual, organization, brand, or entity claiming to represent it.

The `Verified` status indicates that the domain's claimed association has been reviewed and confirmed.

## Verification Requirements

Verification does not require identity documents, passports, certificates, or payment.

The domain owner must provide a publicly accessible reference demonstrating their association with the domain.

Acceptable references may include:

- An official website.
- An official social media account.
- A publicly recognized account or platform.
- Another credible public resource controlled by the person or organization claiming the domain.

The reference must explicitly identify the PDN domain NFT, preferably by its token ID, and request confirmation of its authenticity.

## Verification Procedure

1. The domain owner publishes the domain NFT token ID on an official public resource associated with their identity or organization.
2. A verification request is submitted for the corresponding PDN domain. (Official e-mail Petoron)
3. The submitted public reference is reviewed to establish the connection between the claimant and the domain.
4. Once approved, the domain's `Verified` status is recorded on-chain.

## No Paid Verification

PDN Verification is not a purchasable service.

No payment, subscription, premium membership, or commercial arrangement grants the `Verified` status.

Verification is granted exclusively on the basis of confirmed authenticity and compliance with the verification procedure.

## Important Distinction

A `Verified` status confirms that the association between a domain and its claimed owner or organization has been reviewed.

The verification status is associated with the PDN NFT token and remains attached to that token when ownership is transferred.

In some cases, the owner can send a request to revoke the domain verification; only the owner and no one else can do this.
