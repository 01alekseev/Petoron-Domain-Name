# Petoron Domain Name (PDN) Ownership and Usage Rights

## Independence from PetoronAI Licenses

Petoron Domain Name (PDN) is an NFT-based digital domain name within the Petoron ecosystem. PDN is an official PetoronAI software component.

The PDN software implementation, source code, and related technical components remain subject to the applicable PetoronAI Community License (PCL) or PetoronAI Commercial License (PCL-C). This licensing applies exclusively to the software itself and does not extend to individual PDN NFT domains acquired by users.

## Commercial Deployment Of PDN Software

Notwithstanding the general licensing terms applicable to PetoronAI software, any company, commercial organization, or business may independently install, run, host, and operate the PDN software interface and its supporting application components on its own computers, workstations, or servers, including for internal business operations and commercial environments, without obtaining a PCL or PCL-C license solely for that deployment or operation.

This permission includes operating a locally or privately hosted PDN interface and the supporting components required to make that interface available to the organization's personnel. Such deployment or operation, by itself, does not constitute a use requiring a PetoronAI Commercial License.

### Permitted Activities Without PCL-C

Any individual, company, commercial organization, nonprofit organization, developer, or other entity may, without obtaining a PCL-C license:

- Connect to and interact with the official PDN smart contracts deployed on a public blockchain.
- Read, query, index, monitor, and process publicly available on-chain data exposed by or associated with those contracts.
- Build, deploy, host, operate, and commercialize independent smart contracts, applications, websites, platforms, APIs, interfaces, protocols, and other infrastructure that interact with, integrate with, or use the functionality or publicly available data of the official deployed PDN smart contracts.
- Create products and services that use PDN domain ownership, verification status, domain records, marketplace data, or other publicly accessible contract functionality.
- Charge fees, generate revenue, offer paid services, and conduct any other lawful commercial activity through independently developed products and infrastructure that interact with the official deployed PDN smart contracts.
- Connect wallets and perform ordinary user transactions through the official PDN smart contracts, including registering or acquiring domains where available, purchasing, selling, transferring, or managing PDN NFTs.

No PCL-C license, separate Petoron permission, or royalty is required solely because an independent product or service connects to, reads from, interacts with, integrates with, or commercially builds upon the official PDN smart contracts already deployed on a public blockchain. This permission applies equally to commercial and non-commercial projects.

### Activities Requiring a Separate Software License

The permissions above concern interaction with and independent development around the official deployed PDN smart contracts. They do not grant rights to copy, modify, fork, redistribute, sublicense, or commercially deploy the PDN software implementation or its source code.

A valid license under the applicable PCL or PCL-C terms is required where a party's activity involves use of the PDN software, source code, or other protected PetoronAI software components in a manner that requires such a license under those terms. This includes, where applicable:

- Copying, modifying, or creating a derivative version of the PDN smart contract source code.
- Deploying an independently created copy, fork, or modified version of the PDN smart contracts.
- Redistributing or sublicensing PDN software, source code, or other protected implementation components.

For clarity, independently writing and deploying a separate application or smart contract that communicates with the official PDN smart contracts through their deployed on-chain interfaces is not, by itself, copying, modifying, or deploying the PDN software implementation and does not require a PCL-C license.

The distinction is between using the official PDN contracts as deployed on the blockchain and using or redistributing the protected PDN software implementation itself. All rights not expressly granted in this section remain subject to the applicable PCL or PCL-C terms.


The PetoronAI Community License (PCL) and PetoronAI Commercial License (PCL-C) do not apply to, restrict, or govern ownership or usage rights associated with any PDN NFT domain acquired by a user.

Ownership and usage rights of PDN NFT domains are independent of the licensing terms applicable to PetoronAI software, tools, and other ecosystem components.

## Domain Ownership

Each acquired PDN domain is represented by an NFT recorded on the blockchain.

The owner of a PDN NFT has the right to use and control their domain for any lawful purpose, including commercial activities, without requiring a PCL or PCL-C license.

The owner is free to:

- Use the domain for personal or commercial purposes.
- Generate revenue and derive commercial value from the domain.
- Sell, purchase, transfer, or otherwise dispose of the domain.
- Publish, promote, or publicly associate content and services with the domain.
- Build websites, applications, services, platforms, and other infrastructure around the domain.
- Integrate the domain into commercial products, businesses, and other projects.

## Ownership Protection

A PDN NFT domain belongs to its current on-chain owner.

Neither Petoron nor any PetoronAI license can independently revoke, confiscate, or transfer ownership of a user's PDN NFT domain.

Ownership can change only through an authorized transaction initiated by the owner or another party explicitly authorized to act on the owner's behalf, in accordance with the smart contract and applicable blockchain rules.

The owner's domain is not subject to recurring licensing fees or commercial restrictions imposed by PCL or PCL-C.

## Final Principle

Your domain. Your property.

A PDN NFT domain is an independently owned digital asset. Its owner is free to use, develop, commercialize, sell, and transfer it without obtaining permission under PetoronAI's software licensing framework.

These rights concern ownership and use of the PDN NFT domain itself and do not grant ownership of Petoron software, trademarks, or other separately protected intellectual property.
